## 0.1.0

Initial release.

- `@Module`, `@Injectable`, `@Provides`, `Bind`, `@Named` and `Scope`.
- `@Api`, the route annotations (`Get`, `Post`, `Put`, `Patch`, `Delete`) and
  the parameter annotations (`Path`, `Query`, `Header`, `Cookie`, `Body`).
- The validators `Range`, `Length`, `Pattern` and `OneOf`.
- No dependencies.
